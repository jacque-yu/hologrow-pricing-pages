HOW YOUR MONTHLY CREDITS WORK

Use the same credits for data services and Pulse

Credits are your monthly service allowance: 20 million with Starter or 50 million with Scale. Use them to keep data updated, run queries, export reports, or work in Pulse, Hologrow’s AI workspace. Pulse uses the same balance, so you can put your allowance toward the work you need.

FAQ

Hologrow pricing: your questions answered

Understand your monthly bill, data storage and shared usage allowance.

How much does Hologrow cost each month?

Starter costs $49 for the first paid month, then $89/month. Scale costs $199/month. Enterprise pricing depends on your requirements. The monthly price includes the storage, credits and team access listed in your plan. All prices are in USD.

What do data storage, credits and seats mean?

Data storage is how much source data you can keep and query, measured in rows. A row is a record from a connected platform, such as an order or advertising record. Credits are the usage allowance for processing data and working in Pulse. A seat gives one team member access. Storage, usage and team size are separate limits.

Can I use my credits in Pulse?

Yes. Pulse is Hologrow’s AI workspace. Your monthly credits can be used for Pulse tasks as well as data syncs, queries and report exports. All of these activities draw from the same balance. Extra credit packs can also be used across data services and Pulse.

Which plan should I choose?

Choose Starter for one person, up to 10 million rows of source data and 20 million monthly credits. Choose Scale for up to 3 people, 25 million rows and 50 million monthly credits. Both include data connection, data preparation and delivery support. Contact sales if you need a custom Enterprise plan.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. You can start a 14-day free trial through the Hologrow app. The Starter price of $49 applies to the first paid month; later months cost $89 each.

Do I pay for every store or connection?

There is no separate per-connection fee for supported platforms. Your plan still has limits on data storage, monthly credits and team access. More connections can mean more data or service usage.

How many credits do data updates use?

Each new or changed record uses about 10 credits, counted once in a month even if it changes several times. For example, 100,000 records use about 1 million credits. Updates draw from the same balance as queries, report exports and Pulse tasks.

Is the first historical data sync free?

Yes. Your first sync of up to 2 years of available history is free. That data still counts toward your storage allowance. The amount of history available depends on the source platform. Later updates use credits, and history is retained for up to 2 years within your plan’s storage limit.

Do I get every usage amount shown in the table?

No. The table shows different ways to use one monthly credit balance. For example, using credits on data syncs leaves fewer credits for reports or Pulse. The amounts are estimates for using all credits on one activity; you can mix activities within the same balance.

What if I need more than my plan includes?

You can add storage for $49 per 10 million rows per month, credits for $5 per 1 million, and team members for $20 per seat per month. Storage is sold in whole 10-million-row packs. These are optional add-ons, separate from your plan’s monthly price.
