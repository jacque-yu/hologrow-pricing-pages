FAQ

A few things worth knowing.

Straight answers about pricing, capacity and everyday usage.

Can I try Hologrow for free?

Yes. Start a 14-day free trial through the Hologrow app. Compare the plans here or contact sales to discuss a custom plan.

How much does Hologrow cost?

Starter costs $89 per month, with a $49 first month that saves $40. From month two, Starter is $89 per month. Scale costs $199 per month. All prices are in USD.

How is the reference value calculated?

We use standard add-on rates: $49 per 10 million rows, $5 per 1M credits and $20 per seat. Starter totals $169 and costs $89 per month, saving $80. Scale totals $432.50 and costs $199 per month, saving $233.50. Scale capacity is valued proportionally at $122.50; actual capacity add-ons are sold in whole 10-million-row packs. These are reference values, not former subscription prices.

What is the difference between Starter and Scale?

Starter includes 10 million source data rows, 20M monthly credits and 1 seat. Scale includes 25 million rows, 50M monthly credits and 3 seats. Both include the same standard connections, data preparation and data delivery support.

What is the difference between data capacity and credits?

Capacity is the number of source business data rows you can retain and query. Credits cover ongoing data services such as syncing new or changed records, queries, report exports and Pulse tasks. M means million. Internal tables Hologrow creates to prepare your data do not count again toward source data capacity.

How are data updates counted?

Each new or changed record uses approximately 10 credits, counted once per month even if that record changes several times. One million monthly deduplicated records use about 10M credits. Usage draws from your included credits first; it is not automatically added on top of your subscription price.

Is historical data sync free?

The initial sync of up to 2 years of available history is free. Historical records still count toward your data capacity, and the history that can be retrieved depends on the source platform’s API. Ongoing new or changed records consume credits, and standard historical retention is up to 2 years within your capacity.

Do unlimited connections mean unlimited usage?

No. Supported account, store and market connections have no per-connection fee, and there is no separate charge per device or MCP credential. People still need the appropriate seats and permissions. Queries and tasks share your plan’s credits, and retained data must fit within your capacity.

Are the credit usage examples separate allowances?

No. Each is a reference estimate for using the entire credit pool on one activity. Actual consumption varies with query scope, report size and task complexity. You can combine activities, but they all draw from the same pool.

Can I add more data capacity, credits or seats?

Yes. Add 10 million source data rows for $49 per month, 1M credits for $5, or an extra team seat for $20 per month. Capacity is sold in whole packs of 10 million rows.
