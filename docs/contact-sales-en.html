FAQ

Before we talk.

A few answers to help you get started.

What should I include in my request?

Your data platforms, approximate data volume, team size and the questions or workflows you want Hologrow to support are a useful starting point.

How is Enterprise priced?

Enterprise is quoted around your required source data capacity, service credits, seats and delivery scope. Contact sales to discuss your requirements.

Can I try Hologrow before contacting sales?

Yes. You can start a 14-day free trial through the Hologrow app. Starter and Scale prices and inclusions are available on the pricing page.
